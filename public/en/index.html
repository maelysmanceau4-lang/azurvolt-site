How to reach us

Tell us what is wrong, where you are and, if you can, send a photo. Yoni is often on a job, so we answer in writing as soon as we can. You can use:

  • WhatsApp, SMS or the request form, with photos;
  • e-mail: contact@azurvolt.fr.

Our website request form is in French, but you can write your message in English.

What we do

Troubleshooting (no power, tripping breaker, socket that gets hot, water heater that no longer heats), consumer unit work, electrical renovation, new circuits and sockets, lighting, towel radiators, air-conditioning cleaning and safety upgrades of older installations. Works are quoted in writing before we start. The quote is free from photos; otherwise a technical visit is charged and deducted from the invoice if you accept.

While you wait

If you smell burning or see smoke or sparks: switch off the main breaker if you can do it safely and do not touch any wire. In immediate danger, call 112.

Prices

Net prices. VAT not applicable, article 293 B of the French tax code. The price is given before we travel. Special equipment and additional work are quoted before anything is done.

A real job: moving a WiFi box

For a customer in Cannes, the WiFi box and its cables were lying on the floor. Yoni put the box on a shelf and created a new socket next to it. He ran the cable through the staircase to connect it to the existing switch: as few holes and as little trunking as possible, for a discreet result.

Your questions

Do you speak English?+

In writing, yes. Send us a message by WhatsApp, SMS, e-mail or the form and we reply in English. Yoni is often on a job, so written messages are the best way to reach us.

How much does a call-out cost?+

Troubleshooting is a fixed price that includes travel, fault finding and the first hour: 110 € in Cannes and Le Cannet, 120 € in Antibes, Mandelieu-la-Napoule, Théoule-sur-Mer, Vallauris and Mougins, 130 € in Grasse. The price is given before anyone travels.

Can I send photos of the problem?+

Yes, and it helps. A photo of the consumer unit, the socket or the fitting lets us prepare the visit and sometimes quote remotely. Please do not touch the installation to take it.

Do you work evenings and Sundays?+

Troubleshooting and emergencies 7 days a week, 8am to 10pm, night subject to availability. The price depends on the time slot and is given beforehand.

I rent or own a holiday home. Can you work while I am away?+

It can be arranged if someone you trust (a neighbour, a relative) opens the door. Tell us when you write.

Is travel included?+

Yes. It is included in the troubleshooting fixed price of your area.

Is the quote free?+

Yes, remotely if your information and photos are enough. Only a technical visit on site, when needed, is charged.

When is a technical visit charged?+

When Yoni has to come and study the works on site: 60 € in Cannes or Le Cannet, 70 € in Antibes, Mandelieu-la-Napoule, Théoule-sur-Mer, Vallauris or Mougins, 80 € in Grasse. If you accept the quote, the cost of the visit is deducted in full from the invoice.

What if more work is needed?+

It is announced and needs your agreement before it is done.

Do you clean air conditioners?+

Yes: filters and front panel, condensate drain, and the outdoor unit when it can be reached safely. 120 € for one unit in Cannes and Le Cannet, 130 € in Antibes, Mandelieu, Théoule-sur-Mer, Vallauris and Mougins, 140 € in Grasse (travel and up to 1 hour included), 60 € for each extra unit. Yoni does not work on the refrigerant circuit: no gas recharge, leak test or leak search. If your unit no longer cools, you need a refrigeration technician.
